# Winterly — Decorate Snowy Cabins for the Holidays

Winterly 9minecraft Mod is a cozy winter expansion that fills your world with festive, snowy decoration and a few handy cold-weather extras. It adds colourful gift boxes you can store items in, twinkling garland lights, snow bricks with matching stairs and slabs, dangling icicles, frozen grass and frozen flowers, a cheerful snowguy, cryomarble tools, and wearable santa hats and scarves - which winter skeletons and zombies will even put on during the holidays.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Tyap-Lyap Modding**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
